# HipLab drill media attribution

All drill videos (`assets/drill_media/hip-*.mp4`), their posters (`.jpg`), the
prototype stills (`assets/hip/posters/`) and the self-test comparison images
(`assets/hip/assessment/`) were rendered with the drill renderer in the
3D-Body project (tools/drill-anim). `hip-glute-max-a` and `hip-flexors-a` are
unchanged copies of FlareLab's 2026-10-13 training-human-v2 library clips
(`glute-max-A`, `hip-flexors-A`). Per-clip source, loop length and poster time
are listed in `tools/hip_content/source/hip-media.json`.

The videos use a modified Snow-derived training character with a compact
deformation rig (modifications made in 3D-Body: training clothing, shoes, a
grey faceless mannequin head, toon lighting and outlines, props and
target-muscle teaching highlights). The head reference is derived from
Blender Studio Human Base Meshes, recorded as CC0 in the 3D-Body project.

Muscle colours indicate training targets, not measured activation.

The Noto Sans SC font (`assets/fonts/NotoSansSC.ttf`) is an unmodified copy of
the Google Fonts release under the SIL Open Font License; see
`NotoSansSC-OFL.txt`.
