Reporting an issue

Include your app version, phone model, steps leading to the issue, and any error message. Remove private notifications, records, and account information from screenshots. Do not send API keys, passwords, verification codes, or unnecessary health information.

Deleting local data

Clear SmartFit's storage in Android app settings, or uninstall it, to remove local app data. Clearing memory, starting a new conversation, and clearing media cache affect their respective data only. System backups must be managed separately in platform settings, and reinstalling may restore previous data. See the Privacy Policy.

Deleting local data on iPhone

Use iOS Delete App to remove the app and its related data from the current device. Offload App keeps documents and data, and Remove from Home Screen only hides the icon. Manage system backups separately; restoring a backup may restore earlier records. New conversation, Clear memory and Clear media cache affect their respective data only. See the iPhone privacy information.

Workouts and online AI

Version 1.0.0 (25) uses local rules and templates, and does not provide online AI replies. Use the available local plans, exercise demonstrations, and guided workout flow. The interface and voice guidance are primarily in Simplified Chinese.

Health and safety

SmartFit is not a medical service. Consult a qualified professional about exercise-related risks. Stop if you feel unwell and seek appropriate help. Do not wait for an email response to address an emergency.
